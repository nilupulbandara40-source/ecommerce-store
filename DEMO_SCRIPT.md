# 5-minute demonstration video plan

Keep the video within 5 minutes and keep your camera on, as required by the supplied rubric.

- **0:00–0:25 — Introduction:** State the project title, your name and registration number, and the problem the store solves.
- **0:25–1:05 — Storefront:** Show product cards, product details, search, category filter and price sorting.
- **1:05–2:15 — CRUD:** Open Manage Products. Create a product, show it in the list, edit it, then delete a separate test product. Briefly show validation by trying a negative price.
- **2:15–3:05 — Cart:** Add a product, open the cart, update the quantity and show subtotal/total.
- **3:05–3:50 — Checkout:** Enter demo customer details and place an order. Explain that this is a simulated checkout and no real payment is taken.
- **3:50–4:25 — Database-backed orders:** Show the new order and its item details in Orders; point out that stock decreased.
- **4:25–5:00 — Technical summary:** Briefly explain Flask, SQLite and the Products/Orders/Order Items relationships. End with limitations and possible future improvements.

Record the actual running app and use your own genuine screenshots in the report. Do not claim tests passed unless you ran them.
